How it works

Selfie in, token out.

Start the verification

Ask your user to take a quick selfie. No documents, no passwords, no additional identifiers. Just a selfie.

Liveness check

Our liveness confirms a real, live human, physically present. Blocking every kind of capture fraud.

  • Deepfakes

03

Human Token

After the liveness, we turn that biometric into a high precision, anonymous mathematical token, unique to your company. The same person always returns the same token.
